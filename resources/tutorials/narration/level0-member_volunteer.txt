In this database, every person belongs to one of three groups. <break time="0.6s" /> A volunteer gives their time, with no fee. They serve hands-on in a Red Cross unit — emergency response, first aid, and community programmes. <break time="0.5s" /> A volunteer and member does both. They serve as an active volunteer in a Red Cross unit, and they also register as a paying member. <break time="0.5s" /> A supporting member supports our mission financially, without volunteering. They pay a membership fee, but they are not in a Red Cross unit. <break time="0.7s" /> So, ask two questions: is this person in a Red Cross unit, and have they paid a membership fee? The answers tell you which group they belong to.
